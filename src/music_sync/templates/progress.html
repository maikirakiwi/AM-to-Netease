What happens when you stop?

The worker stops at its next safe point, removes songs added by this run, and restores its mapping changes.

If mirror cleanup already removed songs, the worker adds them back. Playlist order may change when songs are restored.

A failed revert is shown here with an option to retry. Keep the service running until reverting completes.
